Control your laptop with your voice, without touching it.

AccessAI is a prototype built for people who cannot use a mouse or a keyboard. You say what you want in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. It repeats the request, asks before it sends anything, and speaks back.

Prototype. Tested by its builder only, not yet with people with motor disabilities. Not a medical device and not an emergency system.

Why this matters

The World Health Organization describes assistive technology as a part of health technology that helps people live healthy, independent and dignified lives (PAHO, 7 Oct 2021).

WHO estimates that over 1 billion people need at least one assistive product and only 1 in 10 has access, because of high prices, low awareness and a shortage of trained staff (WHO, 23 Aug 2019).

AccessAI aims to be low-cost assistive software: an ordinary laptop plus pay-per-use cloud, with no special hardware.

How it works

A cloud brain on AWS. Elastic Beanstalk runs the service. Amazon Bedrock (Claude Haiku 4.5) understands the request in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

A laptop agent. It carries out the steps: open sites, scroll, compose and send Gmail, message WhatsApp contacts by partial name.

Always confirmed. It asks before sending and speaks every reply. A voice extension for WhatsApp Web is an early prototype.

Built with Kiro and AWS for the Zero to Shipped hackathon. Code: github.com/AreebaGhaffar/accessai-agent
